Chamber Positions
Official positions adopted by the Your Chamber Board of Directors. These guide advocacy strategy and public communications. Updated as legislation advances.
Property Tax · State Legislature
🔴 Oppose: Local Bill 4412 — Annual Commercial Property Reassessment
The Your Chamber of Commerce opposes Local Bill 4412 in its current form. Uncapped annual reassessments impose unpredictable cost burdens on small business property owners and commercial tenants, threatening the economic stability of the Downtown corridor. The Chamber urges the Legislature to adopt a cap-based reassessment model consistent with residential property protections.
Adopted by Board: May 20, 2026 · Vote: 12–0Oppose
Permitting Reform · State Legislature
🟢 Support: State Bill 891 — Small Business Permit Streamlining Act
The Your Chamber of Commerce supports State Bill 891 and urges the full Senate to pass this legislation. Consolidating permit applications and establishing a 30-day processing guarantee will materially reduce the administrative burden on small business owners and accelerate new business formation in our county.
Adopted by Board: April 15, 2026 · Vote: 13–0Support
Zoning · The City
🔴 Oppose: Ordinance 22-B — Commerce Ave Residential Rezoning
The Chamber opposes the proposed conversion of mixed-use commercial blocks on Commerce Ave to residential-only zoning. This change would displace established businesses, reduce commercial tax base, and undermine the City's own Downtown Revitalization Plan adopted in 2023. The Chamber requests the City Council reject this ordinance and pursue an inclusive planning process with business stakeholders.
Adopted by Board: May 20, 2026 · Vote: 11–1Oppose
Federal Lending · U.S. Congress
🟡 Monitor: SBA Reauthorization — Oppose Proposed 22% Funding Cut
The Chamber is monitoring the SBA reauthorization process. If proposed cuts advance, the Board will adopt a formal opposition position and coordinate with the State Business Association to engage the full Congressional delegation. Staff briefing scheduled for July board meeting.
Monitoring status adopted: June 1, 2026Monitoring
